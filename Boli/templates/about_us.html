About Us

Our Story

PunyaUday fund was established by {{ mandir.name }} on the day of the 50th Acharya Shree Vidyasagar Maharaj Diksha Divas (30th June 2018) at Sus Gaon, Pune.

The main purpose of this platform is to track funds the temple will receive in future, and to give devotees a convenient way to monitor their donations — avoiding any negligence of funds.

How It Works

Search your pending donations using your registered phone number. All unpaid records will be listed for you.

Once fetched, select a record and use the WhatsApp or SMS link to notify the admin. Please mention the mode of payment (Online or Cash) and if online, the Transaction ID to help avoid discrepancies.
